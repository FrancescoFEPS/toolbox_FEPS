Questions

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser, on your computer. As the list may contain personal data, keep the file you download in a safe place and delete it when you no longer need it.

Does it change my original file?

No. You get a new file; your original is left as it is.

How does “Remove duplicates” decide?

You choose the column (usually the email address) or “the whole row”. Capitals and spaces are ignored when comparing, so “Ann@x.org” and “ann@x.org ” count as the same. The first row is kept and the others are removed; the list of removed rows is shown under the button.

How does the comparison with another list work?

Choose the other file, then the column used to recognise people (usually the email address). That column must exist, with the same title, in both files. You can then remove the people already in the other list, or keep only those.

My dates look like numbers (45123)

Excel stores dates as numbers. In columns whose title contains “date” or “time”, the tool turns them back into dates (2026-11-15).

Which email addresses count as wrong?

Those without an @, without a dot after it, with spaces inside, or with several addresses in one cell. The tool cannot check that a mailbox really exists.

Only the first sheet of my Excel file is read

Yes. If your list is on another sheet, move it to the first sheet, or save that sheet as a CSV file.

Everything runs in your browser: your list, which may contain personal data, never leaves your computer.
