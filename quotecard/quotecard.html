      Questions

      Is my text or photo uploaded anywhere?

      No. The image is made inside your browser, on your computer.

      My quote is long and the letters become small

      The text is made smaller automatically so it fits. Very long quotes become hard to read: shorten the quote, or choose the portrait format, which has more room.

      Which format should I choose?

      Square works almost everywhere. Portrait takes more space in a social media feed. Wide is the format of link previews and fits a newsletter or a web page.

      How is the photo cut?

      It is cut into a circle, around the centre of the picture. Choose a photo where the face is in the middle.

      My logo cannot be seen on the red or dark style

      Keep “Put the logo on a white plate” ticked: the logo is then shown on a small white rectangle.

      Everything runs in your browser: your text, photo and logo never leave your computer.
