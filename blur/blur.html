Blur and hide

Hide faces, names or any detail on an image before you share it.

How it works: you choose an image, drag a rectangle over each part to hide, and download the result. The hidden parts are changed for good in the new image: nobody can get them back.

1

Choose your image

A JPG, PNG or WebP image (a screenshot, a photo…).

2

Hide what must not be seen

How to hide

Now drag on the image (on the right) to draw a rectangle over each part to hide.

    3

    Download

    Your image will appear here. Then drag on it to hide a part.

    The new image also loses the hidden information of the original photo (date, place, camera). Pixelate and black box cannot be undone. A light blur can sometimes be partly reversed, so do not use it for private details.

    Questions

    Is my image uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything happens inside your browser, on your computer.

    Can someone remove the blur or the black box afterwards?

    With the black box, no. With pixelate at a good strength, no. The new image is a flat picture: the hidden pixels are really replaced, not covered by a layer. A light blur is less safe, so use the black box for names, numbers and faces of people who did not agree to be shown.

    Does it work on a PDF?

    Not yet. Take a screenshot of the page, hide what you need here, and use the image.

    I drew a rectangle in the wrong place

    Use “Undo the last one”, or remove it from the list under the rectangles. “Start again” removes all of them.

    Does it work with a finger on a phone?

    Yes: drag your finger over the image to draw a rectangle.

    Everything runs in your browser: your image never leaves your computer.
