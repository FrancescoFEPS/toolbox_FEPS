Questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser, on your computer.

The GIF file is too heavy

Make it shorter, narrower (for example 400 px), or use fewer images per second, and leave “Smoother colours” unticked. A GIF of 3 seconds at 480 px and 10 images per second is usually a few megabytes at most.

The page says my video cannot be read

Your browser cannot open that kind of video. MP4 (H.264) and WebM work almost everywhere. Videos from an iPhone (HEVC) only work in Safari: in another browser, export or convert the video to MP4 first.

Why are the colours a bit different from the video?

A GIF can only show 256 colours. The page chooses the best 256 for your clip. Ticking “Smoother colours” hides the steps in gradients.

Does the GIF have sound?

No, a GIF never has sound.

Can I use the GIF in a Sarbacane newsletter?

Yes, as an image. Keep the file as light as you can: many email programs load heavy GIFs slowly, and some show only the first image.

Everything runs in your browser: your video never leaves your computer.
