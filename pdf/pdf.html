PDF merge and split

Put several PDFs into one, take some pages out of a PDF, or cut a PDF into smaller files.

How it works: you choose what you want to do, add your PDF files, and download the result. Your original files are never changed.

    Good to know
    • PDFs protected by a password cannot be used. Remove the protection first.
    • Very large files (hundreds of pages) can take a little while.
    • Links, bookmarks and forms of the original files may be lost in the result.

    Questions

    Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything happens inside your browser, on your computer.

    The page says a PDF is protected or could not be read

    The file may be protected with a password, or damaged. Open it, remove the protection (or print it to a new PDF), and try again.

    How do I write the pages to keep?

    “2” is page 2. “1-3” is pages 1 to 3. “5-” is page 5 to the end. Combine them with commas: “1-3, 7, 10-”. You can also repeat a page or change the order: “3, 1, 2”.

    What is in the zip file when I split a PDF?

    One PDF per part, named after the original file and the pages it contains, for example report_p1-2.pdf, report_p3-4.pdf.

    Is the quality of my pages changed?

    No. The pages are copied as they are, nothing is compressed or converted to images.

    Everything runs in your browser: your PDF files never leave your computer.
