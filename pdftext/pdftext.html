PDF text cleaner

Paste text copied from a PDF and get it back without the broken lines, ready to paste in WordPress.

How it works: a PDF saves every line of a text on its own, so a copied text is cut in the middle of sentences. The page glues the lines back together and keeps your real paragraphs. It does not change your words.

1

Paste the text

Select the text in your PDF, copy it, and paste it here.

2

Check the options

    What exactly does it change?
    • Lines of the same paragraph are joined with a single space.
    • A blank line in your text stays a paragraph break. Lines that start with a bullet (•, –) stay on their own.
    • Double spaces and spaces at the start or end of a line are removed.
    • Joined letters such as ﬁ and ﬂ become fi and fl, and invisible soft hyphens are removed.
    3

    Copy the result

    Then paste it in WordPress. If a PDF title, page number or footer was copied with the text, delete it by hand.

    Questions

    Why are my sentences cut when I copy from a PDF?

    A PDF is made to be printed, not to be edited: it stores each line of text separately, with no notion of a sentence. When you copy, you get a line break at the end of each line.

    My paragraphs are all stuck together

    Your PDF probably has no blank line between paragraphs. Make sure “Start a new paragraph after a short line that ends a sentence” is ticked. If it still fails, add a blank line by hand where a paragraph should start, in the box in step 1.

    My paragraphs are cut in the wrong places

    Untick “Start a new paragraph after a short line that ends a sentence”. Only blank lines will then separate paragraphs.

    A word has lost its hyphen

    When a word like “well-known” was cut exactly at its hyphen at the end of a line, the page cannot tell it from a word cut in two. Put the hyphen back by hand, or untick the hyphen option and repair the cut words yourself.

    Does it change my text?

    No. It only changes line breaks, spaces and the few things listed under “What exactly does it change?”.

    Is my text sent anywhere?

    No. Everything happens inside your browser and nothing is sent online.

    Everything runs in your browser: your text never leaves your computer.
